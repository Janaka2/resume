Method

How this was counted.

I tagged every phrase that names a quality. A circle’s area is the number of different people who mentioned it: never more than one count per person, and no weighting for how strongly they put it. One sentence can support more than one quality.

The labels are mine. The words behind them are theirs. Four recommendations are public on my LinkedIn profile; two are written references from 2011 that I published on my blog at the time; one is written feedback from my line manager at UBS to my employer. Titles are as shown on LinkedIn today, or as given in the reference.
