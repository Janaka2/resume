# LinkedIn post

Load `brand-voice`. Read the source page; never post about content you have not read.

## Format

```
<Hook: one line, a concrete claim or question, no emoji>

<2-4 short lines: the insight, with one specific detail (a number, a command, a failure mode)>

<1-2 lines: why it matters in regulated / enterprise engineering>

<Link>
#Java #SpringBoot #Kafka  (3-5 hashtags from the site's real topics)
```

- 120-220 words. Line breaks between paragraphs. No bullet walls, no emoji lists, no "excited to share".
- Speak in first person. Credit sources by name when the post builds on someone's work.
- German version: Sie-form, same structure, written fresh rather than translated.
- Short form: 240 characters, hook + link.

## Post types and cadence

| Type | Share | Example hook |
|---|---|---|
| Technical (blog, lab, academy) | 50% | "A dead-letter topic is not a retry strategy." |
| Career narrative | 25% | "Twenty-two years of pager duty taught me one thing about architecture." |
| Products and academy mission | 25% | "I built a fasting tracker that never phones home. Here is why." |

Best slots (CET): Tue–Thu 07:30–08:30 or 12:00–13:00. Never two product posts in a row.
