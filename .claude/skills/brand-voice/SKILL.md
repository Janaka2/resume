- Calm, senior, concrete. The tone of a senior engineer on a first call.
- First person on the hub, CV and chatbot. Plain instructional voice in blog, lab and academy.
- Short sentences. Lead with the point, then the evidence.

- No exclamation marks. No "passionate", "cutting-edge", "leverage", "synergy", "rockstar", "ninja", "10x".
- Never describe the chatbot as an AI or assistant in its own voice; it speaks as Janaka.
- German: Sie-form, Standard German, product names and English job titles untranslated.
