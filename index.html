How they were built

One engineer, AI in the loop, shipped.

All five products were specified, designed, built, written and deployed by one person — evenings and weekends — with LLMs used deliberately at each step rather than as a novelty. This is the same practice I bring to regulated engineering work: use the model to move fast, then hold the output to the standard the domain demands.

  • Product scope and interaction flows drafted against a model, then cut back by hand until only what earns its place remains.
  • Implementation in tight generate-and-review loops — I stay the architect and the reviewer, and nothing ships that I cannot explain line by line.
  • Interface copy in two languages, and the visual identity of all five apps, produced with AI assistance and edited to a consistent voice.
  • Blank page to public URL in weeks, not quarters, with no team, no budget and no outside help.

Any of the five could find a better home.

All five were developed independently of my employment, so they are mine to pass on. If one fits a portfolio you are building, I am open to a conversation about acquisition or licensing — source, brand, domain where applicable, and a documented handover.
