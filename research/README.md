# Research Tracking Guide

Use this folder as your structured memory for AI research progress.

## Cadence

- **Weekly:** capture updates.
- **Monthly:** synthesize trends.
- **Quarterly:** adjust strategy.

## Entry format (copy for each update)

```md
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Title
- Type: Paper | Model Release | Benchmark | Tooling | Safety | Regulation
- Area: (from trend map)
- Source: URL
- Why it matters: 2-3 bullets
- Confidence: High | Medium | Low
- Action: Read later | Reproduce | Integrate | Ignore
- Notes: short summary
```

## Recommended signal scoring

Score 1-5 for:

- **Impact:** likely ecosystem effect.
- **Maturity:** research-only vs production-ready.
- **Relevance:** usefulness for your projects.

Priority score = `Impact + Maturity + Relevance`.
