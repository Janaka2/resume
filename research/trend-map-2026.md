# AI Trend Map (Living) - 2026

> Update this document monthly. Keep only areas that matter to your goals.

## Priority tiers

- **Tier 1 (Track Weekly):** high impact + immediate practical value.
- **Tier 2 (Track Monthly):** important, but not urgent.
- **Tier 3 (Track Quarterly):** emerging / speculative.

## Tier 1 - Track Weekly

1. **Agentic workflows and tool-use reliability**
   - Planning, memory, recovery/retry patterns, long-horizon tasks.
2. **Evaluation and observability for LLM systems**
   - Task-level evals, regression tracking, traces, cost/latency/quality.
3. **Multimodal models in production**
   - Image/audio/video understanding + tool orchestration.
4. **RAG 2.0 and retrieval quality**
   - Hybrid retrieval, reranking, grounding fidelity, citation quality.
5. **AI safety and policy alignment in deployment**
   - Guardrails, prompt-injection resilience, red-team workflows.

## Tier 2 - Track Monthly

1. **Small/open model optimization**
   - Distillation, quantization, fine-tuning efficiency.
2. **Coding agents and software engineering automation**
   - Code generation, review, test synthesis, vulnerability analysis.
3. **Enterprise AI platform patterns**
   - Governance, model routing, permissions, compliance controls.

## Tier 3 - Track Quarterly

1. **Embodied and robotics-integrated agents**
2. **Scientific discovery agents**
3. **Economic + regulatory shifts affecting deployment**
