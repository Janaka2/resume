Why privacy by construction matters to a buyer

  • No hosting bill and no on-call. There is no backend to scale, patch or wake up for.
  • No data liability. Nothing personal is stored on your side, which keeps GDPR and Swiss data-protection exposure minimal.
  • No app-store gatekeeping. Progressive web apps install on iOS and Android from a link, and ship updates with a deploy.
  • No framework lock-in. Plain web technology means no upgrade treadmill and a wide hiring pool.

Questions

Before you write.

Can I see the source before deciding?

Yes. The walkthrough step is a read of the actual repository, not slides. For a deeper review before terms are agreed, access can be arranged under a simple confidentiality agreement.

Do the products have users?

All four are live and in use. Because none of them collects analytics, the usage picture comes from install and domain data rather than tracking, and I share it in conversation rather than publishing it.

What is the difference between acquisition and licence?

Acquisition means the product becomes yours: source, brand, domain and the right to take it in any direction. A licence means you operate it, typically under your own brand and domain, while ownership and the right to license to others stays with me.

Is there really no backend?

None. Each app runs entirely in the browser and stores records on the device. Loop and BabyLoop can optionally sync to the user's own Google Drive; that uses an OAuth client you register, so even then no data passes through anyone else's servers.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes. Loop and BabyLoop share an architecture and pair naturally; nüchtern and Daily Momentum suit a wellness or productivity portfolio. Ask about a combined handover.
