Prompt Engineering Cheat Sheet

A practical summary for daily use.

RuleWhy it helps
Define a roleSets the right perspective and expertise level.
Give relevant contextReduces wrong assumptions.
Ask for structureMakes the answer easier to verify and reuse.
Use examples when consistency mattersImproves output format and style matching.
Split plan and executionBetter control for complex tasks.
Ask for uncertainty clearlyReduces false confidence.
Validate the resultCatches obvious misses before you trust the output.
Simple rule: the more important the task, the less you should rely on one-shot prompting.
