Daily Momentum

daily-momentum.com · time tracking · PWA

Live

A quiet record of where the day went. One tap says what you are doing — there is no start button and no stop button, because starting one thing is what ends the last one. Everything else is derived from that.

  • The day as a single strip, the week against the week before, patterns drawn only from what was recorded.
  • A forgotten afternoon comes back later as a question rather than a hole; entries can be moved, split, merged or relabelled.
  • No account, no server, no upload — with export to a backup file or a spreadsheet at any time.

Loop

loop.janaka.me · recurring activities · PWA

Live

A memory for the things that repeat — watering, birthdays, filters, bills. Give an activity a schedule, or simply note when it happened and let the app find the rhythm from what you actually did. Your history can suggest a cadence; you decide whether to follow it.

  • Five quiet categories — Home, People, Wellbeing, Maintenance, Finances — each activity with an attention state, a predicted next occurrence and photos.
  • One daily digest instead of a stream of alerts, with quiet hours it keeps to.
  • Data on the device, or by choice in your own Google Drive; portable backup, CSV export, recovery snapshots and a 30-day trash.

BabyLoop

babyloop.janaka.me · baby & toddler care log · PWA

Live

Keep the little moments, without keeping your phone in your hand. Two taps record a feed, a nap, a temperature or a first smile, and the day assembles itself into a summary a tired parent can actually read.

How they were built

One engineer, AI in the loop, shipped.

All four products were specified, designed, built, written and deployed by one person — evenings and weekends — with LLMs used deliberately at each step rather than as a novelty. This is the same practice I bring to regulated engineering work: use the model to move fast, then hold the output to the standard the domain demands.

  • Product scope and interaction flows drafted against a model, then cut back by hand until only what earns its place remains.
  • Implementation in tight generate-and-review loops — I stay the architect and the reviewer, and nothing ships that I cannot explain line by line.
  • Interface copy in two languages, and the visual identity of all four apps, produced with AI assistance and edited to a consistent voice.
  • Blank page to public URL in weeks, not quarters, with no team, no budget and no outside help.

Any of the four could find a better home.
